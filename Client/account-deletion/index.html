Request deletion of your PIMEC Client Portal account

You can request deletion here without signing in or reinstalling the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email Ian Waite at info@pimecineng.com with the subject PIMEC account deletion request.
  2. Include the email address associated with your portal account. Do not send passwords, verification codes or confidential project files.
  3. PIMEC will verify account ownership before processing the request.

Prepare request email

This opens your email app. You must send the email to submit the request. If it does not open, email the address above manually. Opening this page or the email app does not delete your account.

What happens to your information

Deletion covers your sign-in account and associated profile information, routine messages, booking information and other account data not required for retained records. Shared engagement records and resources are reviewed to protect other participants' records.

Requests will be completed within 90 days after verification, or sooner where applicable requirements demand it.

Records retained

  • Contracts and statements of work: 7 years after the engagement ends, for contractual recordkeeping.
  • Invoices and payment records: 7 years after the transaction, for financial recordkeeping.
  • Engineering documents and deliverables: the period required by applicable professional rules, contracts and insurer requirements. Florida documents bearing a licensee's signature, seal and date, and related calculations, must be retained for at least 3 years from sealing. Longer applicable requirements or legal holds may apply.

Retention applies to the necessary records, not continued access to your deleted account. PIMEC will explain any applicable retention exceptions when handling your request.
