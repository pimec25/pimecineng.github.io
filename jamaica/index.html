Common questions

Planning your first discussion.

What information should we prepare?

Start with your business priorities and the problems you want to solve. Relevant data may include monthly P&L reports, sales by customer and product, inventory, delivery performance, and operating KPIs. Agree the scope and secure sharing arrangements before sending confidential files.

Can the work start with a focused profitability review?

Yes. A focused review can investigate EBITDA leakage, loss-making sales, and 80/20 priorities before defining a broader improvement program. Conclusions depend on the completeness and quality of the data available.

How is support for Jamaica arranged?

PIMEC is based in Oklahoma City. Discuss your Jamaica location, team, and project needs directly with PIMEC to agree delivery arrangements and any site requirements.

How will we measure success?

Agree a baseline and a small set of financial and operating measures at the outset. Distinguish estimated opportunities from implemented actions and finance-validated results.
